# 🔒 10 — RULED: the endurance-programming scope trigger (Stage 7)

**Status: RULING (Simon, 2026-07-16 — delegated: "rule 🔒 10").**
**Authority: closes the last open M6 lock (`M6-PLAN.md` §4; audit 10 §3 Wave F). Decides WHEN the
platform opens the Stage-7 scope — real endurance/aerobic programming (bundled with React
Native/Expo iOS + HealthKit, per CLAUDE.md's Stage-7 definition). Grounded in Art 20 (simplicity
earns its place), Art 7 (minimum effective intervention), Art 8/9 (safety/recoverability override

---

## What 🔒 10 is — and is not

It is **not** a design or an engineering-order call (M6 only *names the seam*; it builds the D6/D8
prerequisite, not endurance sessions). It is the **strategic trigger**: the condition under which we
commit to building a whole new capability the engine does not have today (the engine is GYM-ONLY —
it generates no aerobic/conditioning sessions). Opening it wrong in either direction is costly:
too early burns effort on breadth before the core is proven (Art 20); too late starves the
sport/functional cohorts of the endurance their goals actually need.

## The ruling — the trigger is a CONJUNCTION, not a date

Endurance scope opens only when **ALL** of the following hold. The final "open it now" is Simon's
business-timing call, but it may not be taken before every condition below is true.

1. **The learning loop has PROVEN the gym product — on real data, not in principle.** The D16 loop
   is LIVE (M5-L2, 2026-07-16) but INERT: no athlete has yet accrued the ≥3 real blocks a promotion
   needs. "Proven" means a cohort of real athletes trained multiple blocks, promotions actually
   landed, and a review shows the learned priors **helped or were safely neutral** (adherence /
   outcomes not degraded; demotions caught mispredictions as designed). This is the Wave-F
   "proves the gym product" condition made concrete — an **evidence + time** gate, not a code gate.
2. **M6 is complete — V2 *is* the engine.** The allocator re-seat has landed (🔒 9), and critically
   the **D6 strategy + D8 microcycle typed objects (M6 sub-phase (c)) exist**, plus the widened
   quality vocabulary. These are the *explicit* endurance prerequisite (audit 10 §3): endurance
   periodisation cannot be built on the pre-M6 fragments.
3. **The gym product is commercially validated.** The Individual package (and ideally Team) shows
   real retention/value — demand justifies the breadth expansion (Art 20: a new capability must
   earn its place against the business case, not ambition). Endurance is a large surface (aerobic
   session generation, zone/pace/HR models, concurrent-training periodisation, wearable effort
   ingestion); it should follow proven pull, not precede it.
4. **Endurance is designed to the FULL governance bar BEFORE any build.** A design spec + science
   review — concurrent-training interference (the interference effect on strength), aerobic
   periodisation and zone models, and overtraining/recoverability safety — ratified against the
   Constitution/EDS, diagnosis-first with D14 validation, exactly as every V2 stage. Endurance
   sessions carry real safety load (overtraining, concurrent interference), so this is **Art 8/9
   safety-critical**, never a bolt-on.

## Consequences / posture

- **Not now, and not automatically at M6 exit.** As of 2026-07-16 conditions 1 and 2 both fail (the
  loop is inert; M6 is unbuilt), so endurance is demonstrably not near. This ruling formalises the
  wait and, more importantly, defines what "ready" *means* so the eventual call is evidence-driven,
  not a gut decision under roadmap pressure.
- **M6 continues to only NAME the seam** (build D6/D8, the vocabulary, the load-model hooks a future
  endurance module would consume) — never the endurance sessions themselves.
- **Stage 7 opens as a bundle.** Per CLAUDE.md, Stage 7 is "real endurance programming; React
  Native/Expo iOS + HealthKit." HealthKit/wearable effort data is a natural input to endurance load,
  so the trigger opens the whole stage; the endurance design spec (condition 4) should assume the
  native/wearable substrate is part of the same commitment.
- **Re-evaluation cadence.** The trigger is checked at **M6 exit**, then at each subsequent product
  review, against conditions 1–4 — not on a fixed date.
